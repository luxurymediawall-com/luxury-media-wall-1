    Bespoke Interior Design

    Where function meets architecture

    A media wall is the one surface everybody in the room looks at. We treat it the way an architect treats a facade — proportion first, then material, then light.

    Luxury Media Wall designs and builds bespoke television walls for villas, penthouses and apartments across the UAE. Every scheme starts with a site survey and a set of drawings, and ends with a wall where the joints line up, the lighting is balanced and not a single cable is visible. Marble, travertine, oak, walnut, fluted panelling, backlit onyx — cut, finished and installed by our own team.
