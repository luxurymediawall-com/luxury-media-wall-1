Privacy policy

This page explains what personal details Luxury Media Wall collects when you contact us, why we collect them and what we do with them. We only use your details to answer your enquiry and deliver your project.

What we collect

  • Details you send us: your name, phone number, email, area, wall size, photos and message, when you use our enquiry form, a Google ad form, WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Website usage: anonymous information such as pages visited and buttons tapped, through Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta pixel, using cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a design and quotation, and arrange the site survey and installation.
  • To measure which of our adverts and pages work, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your details and we do not share them with anyone for their own marketing.

Services that handle your details

  • WhatsApp (Meta) for messages you send us.
  • Google (Analytics, Ads and ad lead forms) and Meta (pixel) for website and advert measurement.
  • FormSubmit, which emails us a copy of the enquiry form so no enquiry is lost.
  • Google Workspace for our email.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry or project, and for our business records after that. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser settings.
