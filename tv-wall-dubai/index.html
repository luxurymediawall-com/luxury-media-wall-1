What a TV wall can include

Feature cladding

Marble, porcelain stone, travertine, fluted oak or walnut behind and around the screen.

Built-in storage

Closed cabinets for the router, receiver and consoles, with ventilation so nothing overheats.

Floating TV unit

A wall-hung console under the screen that leaves the floor clear.

LED lighting

Backlit onyx, lit niches and soft perimeter lighting on a dimmer.

Hidden cables, every time

Power, HDMI and network runs go inside the wall structure, and the devices sit in closed storage. Once the wall is finished, no cable is visible.

TV wall questions

How much does a TV wall cost in Dubai?

It depends on the size, the material and how much storage and lighting it includes. Fluted panelling and lacquer cost less than marble or backlit onyx. Send the wall width, height and a photo on WhatsApp and we reply with an estimate the same day.

Can you build a TV wall in a rented apartment?

Often, yes. A panelled wall can be fixed with minimal changes to the original wall. Check your tenancy terms first, and we can advise at the survey.

What is the difference between a TV wall and a media wall?

People use both names. A TV wall is usually the decorative cladding around the screen; a media wall adds storage, shelving and cable management as well. We build both.
